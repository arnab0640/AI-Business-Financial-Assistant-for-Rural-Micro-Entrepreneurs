SNU BizSathi Executive Problem Statement Brief

MoSJE Priority Solution

Problem Objective

Develop an AI-powered hyper-local advisory platform for rural micro-entrepreneurs to eliminate predatory informal lending, automate MoSJE scheme subvention matching, and enable bankable loan applications.

Target Beneficiaries

Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), Safai Karamcharis, and Women Self-Help Groups (SHGs) under NSFDC, NBCFDC, NSKFDC, and PM-DAKSH.

Key Innovation

Multilingual Voice AI (7 Languages), Web Speech TTS audio readout for illiterate artisans, real-time market demand index, and 1-click Bankable Detailed Project Report (DPR) generation.

1. SNU BizSathi AI Business Advisory Copilot

Ask any question about starting a rural business, raw material procurement, product pricing, or MoSJE loan applications in your mother tongue.
